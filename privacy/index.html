Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Version 1.0
Operator: junxiuli · Contact: matt1232026@gmail.com

Your photos are analyzed on your device. Optional usage sharing is off by default. Subscription information is processed separately to provide and restore paid access.

1. Who is responsible

HueMuse is operated by junxiuli, who is responsible for the personal information processed for the purposes described in this policy. Contact matt1232026@gmail.com for privacy questions, requests, or complaints. This policy covers the HueMuse application, related service interfaces, and these policy pages. Apple and other independently operated services may also process information under their own notices.

HueMuse does not require a user account. Random installation and subscription identifiers are pseudonymous identifiers, not a guarantee of anonymity. This policy distinguishes information that stays on your device from information processed by service providers.

2. Photos, color profiles, and local information

Face and clothing photos are analyzed on your device. HueMuse does not upload the photos, facial pixels, image URLs, raw color samples, or facial measurements to our analytics service. Your saved profile photo, season selection, palette, scan images, scan results, and saved colors are stored in the application's local storage.

Photo analysis supports color guidance and photo-quality checks, not identification of a person. We do not use it for face recognition, identification across applications, or medical diagnosis. Local processing may evaluate framing, lighting, and image quality. Results can still be personal information even though they are not transmitted to us.

When you choose an export, save-to-Photos, or sharing action, iOS or the destination you select handles the resulting copy. Your device backup settings may include application data in an operating-system backup. Those copies are controlled by your system settings and the destination provider.

3. Permissions and information we do not request

Camera access is used to capture a photo when you request a photo feature. Photo library access or the system photo picker lets you choose an image; permission to add images is used when you request an export to Photos. You may revoke permissions in iOS Settings, although the related function may then be unavailable.

The current application does not ask for your contacts, precise location, payment card details, or a HueMuse account password. It has no advertising SDK or cross-application advertising tracking. We do not use your photos to train a remote AI model. If the product's practices change, we will update the notice and obtain any required permission before the new processing begins.

4. Optional usage information

Optional usage sharing is off by default and depends on a separate setting. If you enable it, HueMuse can send a random installation ID, an anonymous RevenueCat user ID, event IDs, event names, event times, and limited feature properties to our Cloudflare-hosted service. Examples include a selected season, match score, option selected, product ID, or the feature from which a purchase was started.

The server restricts properties to an allowed set. Analytics events do not contain photos, photo URLs, facial pixels, or raw color samples. Usage events may describe onboarding, analysis, clothing scans, saved items, and purchase or restore steps. We use this information to understand feature use, diagnose problems, and improve the application. Linking an analytics event to a subscription identifier does not make it a complete accounting record.

5. Purchases and subscription information

Apple processes payments and refunds. RevenueCat manages subscription entitlements and purchase restoration. Processing can include a pseudonymous app user ID, product and transaction identifiers, purchase or expiry dates, store and subscription status, and device or application information used by the subscription SDK, such as operating system and application version.

Our service receives subscription notifications and checks the authoritative subscription state with RevenueCat to maintain access and prevent duplicate processing. The precise information available depends on the purchase and SDK configuration. Optional usage consent does not disable subscription processing needed to deliver, verify, or restore a purchase. We do not receive your full payment card number.

6. Technical and security information

Network service providers necessarily handle connection information, such as an IP address, request time, device or browser information, and requested resource, when a request reaches their infrastructure. They may keep their own operational or security logs. These technical records are distinct from optional product analytics.

Our analytics endpoint uses a SHA-256 digest of the connection IP address, or an installation-ID digest when the address is unavailable, for temporary rate limiting. Its rate-limit database does not store the original IP address. Hashing does not necessarily make a record anonymous. Installation credentials are checked using stored digests to authorize activity deletion and prevent unauthorized requests.

7. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and any diagnostic or purchase details you choose to provide. We use them to answer your request, verify a claim where necessary, investigate an issue, and keep a record of its resolution. Email providers also handle the message under their own arrangements.

Provide only what is needed. Do not email payment card numbers, passwords, or sensitive photographs. If a screenshot is necessary, remove unrelated personal information first. We do not add you to a marketing mailing list simply because you contact support.

8. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract for processing necessary to provide the requested application functionality and subscription access. We rely on your consent for optional usage analytics. You can withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

We rely on legitimate interests for proportionate service security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and handling ordinary support communications, balanced against your privacy rights. Where processing is legally required, such as responding to a lawful request or keeping required transaction records, we rely on the relevant legal obligation. Legal claims may justify preserving only information needed for that claim.

The information needed for a purchase or support request is required only for that function. You can use available free functions without enabling optional analytics. Refusing optional consent does not itself remove paid entitlements.

9. Who receives information

Apple receives purchase, payment, store, and device information under its own arrangements. RevenueCat receives information needed for subscription administration. Cloudflare provides the network and database infrastructure for optional usage events and subscription-service functions. The email provider involved in a support message processes communications. These recipients do not receive your local photos from HueMuse's analytics flow.

Providers may process information on our behalf for contracted functions or independently for their own payment, security, or legal duties. Their respective notices explain those independent activities. We may disclose limited information when legally required, to investigate a substantiated security issue, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

If the operation of HueMuse is transferred, information necessary for continued service may be transferred with appropriate protections and any legally required notice. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not authorize providers to use local photos for their own marketing.

10. International processing

Depending on your location and the providers' infrastructure, subscription information, optional events, technical records, and support communications may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Privacy protections may differ between countries. Local photos remain on your device unless you separately export or back them up through system services.

Where European or UK transfer restrictions apply, restricted transfers require an appropriate legal mechanism, such as an applicable adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards, together with any supplementary measures required for the circumstances. This policy does not represent that a particular certification or contract covers every provider or request. Contact us for information about the destinations and safeguards applicable to your data, and to request a copy or description of relevant safeguards subject to lawful redactions.

11. How long information is retained

Local photos and results remain until you remove them, remove application data, or your device storage is cleared. Independently maintained exports and backups follow your system or destination settings.

Optional server usage events have a 90-day retention window. Daily cleanup can introduce up to one additional scheduled cleanup cycle. The client queue is limited to the latest 200 pending events. Turning off usage sharing clears queued optional events; it does not by itself delete events already received by the server.

Subscription notification deduplication records have a 180-day retention window, subject to the daily cleanup cycle. Current entitlement and synchronization records are maintained for purchase administration and restoration; they are separate from the optional-event retention schedule. Necessary legal or financial records may be retained for the applicable legal period. Contact us about a particular purchase record or deletion request.

Rate-limit buckets are cleaned daily after a 24-hour window, resulting in approximately 24–48 hours of storage under normal scheduling. Installation authorization records and revoked credential digests are retained to authenticate requests and prevent deleted activity from being reintroduced. Support information is kept while resolving your request and as reasonably necessary for follow-up or a legal obligation. Provider-managed technical logs have their own retention arrangements; we do not promise that those are deleted by an in-app action.

12. Your in-app controls

You can retake or remove saved personal photos, clear local application data, and manage saved items using the available controls. Clearing HueMuse's local data removes its stored copies; it does not remove originals from Photos, copies sent to another application, device backups, or Apple subscriptions.

You can turn optional usage sharing off at any time. Use "Delete optional usage activity" in the privacy controls to request removal of server events linked to the current installation. The service retains a revocation marker to reject late events from the revoked credentials. Check the displayed success or error result rather than assuming a request succeeded.

If reinstalling has removed the old installation identifier, the new installation cannot necessarily identify old activity. Contact us for assistance; we may need proportionate information to find a record. We will not ask you to create a HueMuse account solely to submit a privacy request.
